Notes to remember for Assignment 6: 
You can kill a process like this
Make sure to kill aesdsocket and valgrind before starting, they may be in the background
the full-test.sh script isn't always clean

# Source - https://stackoverflow.com/a/3510850
# Posted by paxdiablo, modified by community. See post 'Timeline' for change history
# Retrieved 2026-02-22, License - CC BY-SA 4.0
kill $(ps aux | grep '[a]esdsocket' | awk '{print $2}')
kill $(ps aux | grep '[v]algrind' | awk '{print $2}')

If that isn't enough (ie. valgrind is stuck), 
call with kill -9 to send SIGKILL
kill -9 $(ps aux | grep '[a]esdsocket' | awk '{print $2}')
